# MapForMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

[日本語](privacy) · **English** · [한국어](privacy-ko)

MapForMe ("the App") is designed to respect your privacy and to collect no more information than it needs. This policy explains what information the App handles, what it is used for, and what choices you have.

## 1. Accounts

The App **does not require an account**. The developer does not collect or store information that identifies you personally, such as your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth.

## 2. Information stored on your device

Everything you create or save in the App is **stored only on your device**. It is never sent to, or stored on, the developer's servers.

- Saved places (name, address, coordinates, notes, status)
- Tags, lists, and plans (itineraries)
- Photos attached to places
- App settings

This data is erased when you delete the App from your device. You can also erase it from **Settings → Delete all data** inside the App.

## 3. Information sent outside the App

### 3-1. Usage data (analytics)

To improve the App, anonymous usage data is sent to the analytics service **PostHog** (servers in the EU region).

What is sent is **only a record of actions and counts** — which features were used, and how far. The content you enter or create is never sent. The App also disables GeoIP, the feature that estimates a location from the sender's IP address.

**What is sent:**

- An anonymous identifier generated by the App (a random value per device; it is not linked to your name or email address)
- Events of the following kinds:
  - **Screen views**: which screen was opened (the screen name only; no identifiers of the places or spots being shown)
  - **Subscription actions**: opening the plan screen, switching between monthly and annual, tapping the purchase button, completing a purchase
  - **Usage-limit records**: reaching a monthly usage limit, reaching the retry limit for a search
  - **Import statistics**: how far you progressed through the import guide, how many places were imported and how many were sorted automatically, and the Google category names of places that received no tag
  - **Location-fix records**: opening the fix screen, and whether the fix completed (outcome only)
  - **Search completion**: that a search was confirmed, and how many times candidates were fetched
- Device type and manufacturer, OS name and version, App version and build number, whether it is running on a simulator, and the device's language and time zone settings

**What is never sent:** the names, addresses, or coordinates of your saved places, your photos, your notes, the text you type when searching or fixing a location, the names of your lists and tags, and your GPS location are **never sent**. The App does not record your screen, and it does not capture your interactions automatically (autocapture).

The kinds of events sent may change as features are added or improved. Even when kinds are added, the scope of "what is never sent" above will not change. If that scope ever changes, this policy will be revised.

**How to stop it:** turn off **Settings → Send usage data** in the App and sending stops immediately. You can change this at any time.

### 3-2. Searching for and importing places

When you search for a place, the text you entered and your display language (Japanese, English, or Korean) are sent to the **Google Places API**. When you then select a suggestion, only the identifier of that suggestion is sent to retrieve its details.

When the App fills in the address and coordinates of an imported place, the **name and address** of that place are sent to the **Google Geocoding API** (the language is fixed to Japanese for this request).

**Your GPS location is not sent.** Search suggestions are accompanied by a fixed area covering Japan, and address completion by a preference for Japan; both are fixed values and have nothing to do with where you are.

### 3-3. Purchase information

The App uses **RevenueCat** and **Apple** to manage the status of subscription purchases, renewals, and cancellations. Payment details such as credit card numbers are handled by Apple, and **the developer never receives them**.

## 4. Use of device features

| Feature | Purpose | Sent outside |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Used only when you tap "current location" on the map, to centre the map | **No** (used on the device only) |
| Photos and camera | Used to choose or take photos to attach to a place | **No** (stored on the device) |

Even if you deny the location permission, all of the App's main features — showing the map, saving places, and organising them — remain available.

## 5. Tracking and advertising

The App **shows no advertising**. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The developer does not attempt to identify individual users from the information collected.

## 6. Retention and deletion

- Data on your device: erased by deleting the App, or from **Settings → Delete all data**
- Usage data: turning off **Settings → Send usage data** stops any further sending. If you would like data that has already been sent to be deleted, please contact us at the address below
- Purchase history: managed in accordance with the policies of Apple and RevenueCat

## 7. Third-party services

Please refer to the privacy policies of the external services the App uses:

- PostHog: <https://posthog.com/privacy>
- Google (Places API / Geocoding API / Maps SDK): <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- RevenueCat: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>
- Apple (App Store / in-app purchases): <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

## 8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed primarily at children. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the last-updated date revised. If the change is significant, it will be announced in the App.
